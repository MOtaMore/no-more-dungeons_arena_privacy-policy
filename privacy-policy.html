Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026 • VERSION 1.0
Privacy Summary: No More Dungeons - Arena does not collect sensitive personal data, phone numbers, email addresses, financial info, or GPS coordinates. Your game progress is handled locally on your device or via ephemeral multiplayer network sessions without commercial ad trackers or third-party data broker sales.

1. General Information & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes data handling practices for the video game No More Dungeons - Arena (hereinafter, "the Game"), developed and maintained as an open-source project hosted on GitHub: https://github.com/MOtaMore/no-more-dungeons_arena.

By accessing, installing, or playing No More Dungeons - Arena, you accept the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect (and What We Do NOT Collect)

A. Data we do NOT collect:

  • We do NOT request legal names, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • We do NOT require passwords, personal emails, or mandatory accounts.
  • We do NOT process financial details, bank accounts, or credit card information.
  • We do NOT perform GPS or precise geolocation tracking.
  • We do NOT integrate third-party commercial behavioral advertising networks.

B. Data processed locally on your device:

  • Warrior Handle / Callsign: The nickname you voluntarily choose is stored locally in your browser storage (LocalStorage).
  • Gameplay Preferences: Audio settings (muted or active), chosen armor color, and gladiator class.
  • Scores & Custom Maps: Custom levels forged in the 3D editor and session high scores.

3. Local Storage (LocalStorage) & Cookies

The Game utilizes standard browser LocalStorage to persist your preferences across sessions without account creation. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies or commercial advertising cookies.

You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing your browser cache or site data in your browser settings.

4. Multiplayer Features & Network Traffic

In online multiplayer modes, the Game utilizes real-time communication protocols (WebSockets and WebRTC Peer-to-Peer):

  • Ephemeral packets transmitting 3D coordinates, orientation, weapon fire events, and frags are shared between peers during the active match.
  • IP addresses may be processed ephemerally by signaling servers and peer-to-peer protocols strictly to establish network connections during gameplay.
  • No private communication histories or chat logs are retained for analytics or sold to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child safety standards. Because the Game does not collect personally identifiable information, it provides a safe environment for players of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. User Rights (GDPR / CCPA / International Regulations)

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right of access and rectification: Modify your handle and preferences anytime directly inside the game menus.
  • Right to erasure: Clearing your browser's local cache immediately and permanently deletes all data associated with the Game.
  • Non-sale of data: No More Dungeons - Arena has never sold, does not sell, and will never sell personal user data.

7. Data Security

Static game content is served through encrypted HTTPS connections, ensuring secure delivery and protection against tampering.

8. Hosting & Third-Party Infrastructure

The Game documentation is hosted on GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP addresses and timestamps) for security and content delivery. For details, refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if technical or regulatory requirements evolve. Any revisions will appear on this page with an updated timestamp.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding privacy in No More Dungeons - Arena, please submit an issue on our official GitHub repository:

https://github.com/MOtaMore/no-more-dungeons_arena/issues
